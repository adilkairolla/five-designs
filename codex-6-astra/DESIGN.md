# Ratio Reader design plan

Five independent visual identities share a working PDF preview and sample reader.

1. The margin: paper #f7f5ef, ink #242522, cobalt #254ce8, annotation #e7ebfc, rule #d9d8d0. DM Sans interface with Instrument Serif editorial headlines. Left-aligned promise faces a layered, annotated document. `[copy | source → insight]`. The document itself is the illustration; margin annotations explain the product.
2. After hours: indigo #171a36, violet #a8a0ff, peach #ffb995, white #f3f0ff, slate #9294b0. Space Grotesk display and DM Sans body. Centered headline above a luminous circular reading lens. `[headline / document → lens → understanding]`. An optical metaphor makes the reading transformation tangible.
3. Study club: apricot #ffad85, grape #563768, butter #fff0b2, violet #d9c7f2, ink #35223f. Fraunces expressive headlines and DM Sans body. Oversized left-aligned headline paired with a playful book character and floating flashcards. `[big promise | book + flashcards]`. Friendly school stationery makes difficult material approachable.
4. The essential: white #f4f4f0, tomato #ef442f, black #242421, gray #deded7, soft red #ffdfd9. Space Grotesk display and body. Giant left-aligned typography, a split editorial grid, and a document compression graphic. `[READ / LESS. | source stack → essential sheet]`. The ratio between information volume and meaning is the graphic concept.
5. A little clarity: linen #f1f0e8, forest #314537, sage #dfe4d5, moss #77836a, white #fafaf4. Instrument Serif headline and DM Sans body. Wide nature photograph with centered reading invitation and a quiet reading note. `[landscape + invitation / reading card]`. Space, daylight, and the outdoors connect less reading friction to more time to think.

Review: revised the initial dark concept from standard black/green to indigo/peach, with an optical lens specific to reading. Each page has a different hero composition, graphic language, typography, content rhythm, and lower-page layout. Numbering is reserved for actual steps. No fabricated customer counts or testimonials.
